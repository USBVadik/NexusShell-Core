Production workflow / AI filmmaking

Second Unit

An agentic film crew, from brief to final cut.

Product page ↗
Production workflowExplore the method
01 / 06 · Prepare

Give the film a direction.

Start with the brief, budget and acceptance criteria. Analyse references for composition, light, colour and rhythm, then turn those observations into a story and a shot plan.

Starts with
Brief, constraints, budget
Leaves behind
Measured refboard and story beats
Review
Does each shot have a purpose, a clear action and a place in the story?
Your decision
Approve the direction and story.
02 / 06 · Establish

Keep a shared picture of the scene.

Approve character and location masters. Record the features that must remain consistent, the action axis, light direction and prop states. Each new shot starts from its master.

Starts with
Approved story and references
Leaves behind
Character masters, canon and scene map
Review
Character features, screen direction, light and prop scale stay consistent.
Your decision
Approve the masters and keyframes.
03 / 06 · Generate

Explore in drafts. Keep the accepted take.

Build keyframes with a declared camera, subject, action and change over time. Explore motion in inexpensive drafts. Keep accepted takes as source files for the final edit and upscale.

Starts with
Approved keyframe and job specification
Leaves behind
Motion drafts and contact sheets
Review
Inspect the usable window, physical contact, faces, hands and movement.
Your decision
Choose the take that belongs in the cut.
04 / 06 · Review

Judge the shot inside the film.

Inspect contact sheets and details, then watch shots inside the cut. Separate reviewer agents check framing and physical contact, continuity and motivation, and colour across the sequence.

Starts with
Draft takes, scene map and director’s notes
Leaves behind
Timecoded findings and a revised cut
Review
A plausible frame can still fail in motion or at the next cut.
Your decision
Accept, redirect or request another pass.
05 / 06 · Finish

Finish the version you approved.

Assemble the edit with versioned scripts. Match colour outliers, apply the look, and add grain at delivery size. Check the exported file and keep a no-music version where required.

Starts with
Approved cut and accepted source takes
Leaves behind
Delivery master and rebuild records
Review
Review the whole sequence, export metadata and delivery format.
Your decision
Sign off on the cut, look and delivery.
06 / 06 · Carry forward

Make the next film start with more context.

Record rejected takes, the reason, the proposed fix and whether it worked. Accepted references and A/B preferences become material for the next review. Proven fixes move into the shared rules.

Starts with
Failures, accepted shots and A/B preferences
Leaves behind
Relevant fixes, review rules and regression references
Review
A correction keeps its outcome and the feedback that caused it.
Your decision
Set the taste through concrete verdicts.
Human approval at the creative decisions.Each film feeds the next ↶
