Review is part of the workflow.

Separate reviewer agents inspect the work. The director approves the keyframes and the cut before accepted takes move to delivery.

CinematographyDP / takesDoes the frame hold up?+

Perspective, light, depth, character features, contact with the ground, hands and prop scale. A shot can be rejected, returned for another take, or passed to the director.

EditingEDDoes one shot lead to the next?+

What motivates an action, what causes the next event, and whether continuity survives the cut. The director’s notes are tracked against timecodes.

ColourCODoes the sequence belong together?+

Match before applying the look. Inspect skin and the full sequence as well as individual cuts. Numeric comparisons support the review; the director decides whether the film looks right.

The models are not fine-tuned on the reference library. Craft knowledge is recorded as rules, checklists and measured observations used around the models.

A correction from Lawn Wars, a comedy about two suburban dads racing ride-on mowers at 7 a.m.

Production noteLawn WarsColour / r6 → r6b

The r6 cut met the local colour-difference targets, yet the sequence drifted between warm and cool shots. A second pass pulled the whole cut toward one target.

The author rejected it: the image had become cold and the greens had lost separation. A warmer uniform pass was rejected too. The final returned to the earlier grade.

The rule that changed

Match the outliers.
Review the whole film.

Correct outliers selectively and inspect the full sequence as a contact sheet before chasing a single target. A good measurement still needs a creative verdict.

What the next film remembers.

    Try

  3. 03

    Promote

  4. 04

    Retrieve

    Relevant fixes and accepted examples inform the next job.

Accepted shots form regression references; recorded A/B preferences help calibrate reviewers. This loop updates instructions and review material, not the weights of the generation models.

Before a job runs

Declare the model, inputs, estimated cost and remaining budget. The runner checks spending caps and request fingerprints before submission.
